Copy a view as a Mermaid diagram, for pasting into a PR, a doc or a ticket. The Storybook graph and the Payload Lineage page copy a flowchart of what they show; the Data model page copies an `erDiagram` in crow's-foot notation. The Payload plugin also exports `contentGraphToMermaid` and `schemaToMermaid` for scripts.
